# Delivery and Warranty Terms

## 1. Delivery requirements

### 1.1 Standard delivery limit

The standard maximum delivery time is 45 calendar days from purchase order issue. A quotation above 45 days is ineligible unless an exception is approved under POL-001.

### 1.2 Delivery evidence

A quotation must state delivery days as a whole number. The recommendation records the quoted delivery days and compares them with the request constraint.

### 1.3 Partial delivery

A supplier may propose partial delivery only when the request allows it. Each partial delivery must state quantity, date, and receiving location.

### 1.4 Late delivery

A delivery more than 7 calendar days late must be recorded as an incident. Repeated late delivery may reduce the supplier's score or trigger a status review.

## 2. Quantity and acceptance

### 2.1 Available quantity

The quoted available quantity must be at least the requested quantity at the time of recommendation. A lower quantity is insufficient even if the supplier could restock later.

### 2.2 Inspection

The receiving team checks quantity, model, serial numbers, condition, and accessories against the purchase order. Discrepancies are recorded before acceptance.

### 2.3 Acceptance period

Acceptance testing must be completed within 5 business days of delivery. A defect found during acceptance is handled as a warranty claim.

## 3. Warranty

### 3.1 Minimum warranty

The minimum warranty is 12 months for furniture and office supplies, 24 months for electronics, and 36 months for IT hardware including laptops.

### 3.2 Warranty start

The warranty period starts on the acceptance date, not the invoice date or manufacture date.

### 3.3 Repair or replacement

The supplier must repair or replace a defective item within 10 business days. If repair is not possible, the supplier must provide an equivalent replacement.

### 3.4 Warranty exclusions

Accidental damage, unauthorised modification, and normal consumable wear may be excluded only if the quotation states the exclusion clearly.

## 4. Returns and refunds

### 4.1 Dead-on-arrival

A dead-on-arrival item must be replaced at no cost. The supplier bears return shipping for confirmed defects.

### 4.2 Incorrect item

An incorrect model, quantity, or specification is treated as a supplier error and must be corrected within 10 business days.

### 4.3 Refund

If replacement is not possible, the organisation may request a refund equal to the invoiced amount for the affected items.

## 5. Category notes and near-miss cases

### 5.1 Electronics

Electronics quotations must state the model and warranty period. A quotation without a model identifier is incomplete.

### 5.2 Furniture

Furniture quotations must state dimensions and assembly requirements. Delivery and installation count toward the 45-day limit.

### 5.3 IT hardware

IT hardware must be delivered with the required power accessories and warranty documentation. Laptop deliveries must include the exact model and serial-number list.

### 5.4 Accessories are not laptops

Laptop bags, docking stations, external monitors, and replacement chargers are accessories, not laptop purchases. They follow their own product category and approval rules unless they are bundled into the same laptop quotation.

### 5.5 Near-miss example

A request for 500 laptop bags is an office supplies purchase, not a 500-laptop purchase. The laptop quantity thresholds in POL-002 do not apply to that request.
