# Contracting Guidelines

## 1. When a contract is required

### 1.1 Value threshold

A written contract is required for any purchase above INR 5,00,000, any purchase with a delivery period above 30 days, or any purchase involving access to confidential data.

### 1.2 Standard terms

The contract must reference the supplier ID, product or service scope, quantity, unit price, currency, delivery date, warranty, and payment terms.

### 1.3 Purchase orders

A purchase order may be used below the contract threshold when the supplier accepts the standard terms in writing. The purchase order is not a substitute for required approvals.

## 2. Legal review

### 2.1 Mandatory review

Legal review is mandatory above INR 25,00,000, for non-standard liability terms, and for any contract involving restricted data.

### 2.2 Review record

The legal review record must identify the reviewer, review date, contract version, and any approved deviations.

### 2.3 Signature authority

Only an authorised signatory may sign a contract. The signatory must confirm that the required procurement and finance approvals are recorded.

## 3. Required clauses

### 3.1 Data protection

A contract involving personal data must include the data protection addendum in POL-010 or terms at least as protective.

### 3.2 Security

A contract involving systems access must include the security obligations in POL-005, including the 24-hour incident notification.

### 3.3 Delivery and warranty

The contract must include the delivery limit, acceptance process, warranty period, and remedy for late or defective delivery.

### 3.4 Termination

The contract must state termination rights for material breach, insolvency, and a sustained failure to meet security obligations.

### 3.5 Audit

The organisation may audit compliance with the contract on reasonable notice. Audit rights must not require access to unrelated supplier data.

## 4. Contract changes

### 4.1 Variation

A change to scope, price, delivery, or liability requires a written variation approved at the same level as the original commitment.

### 4.2 Renewal

Automatic renewal is prohibited unless the renewal value and notice period are stated in the contract and approved by procurement.

### 4.3 Price changes

A price increase after quotation selection requires a new quotation and approval. The original recommendation cannot be reused.

## 5. Records

### 5.1 Repository

The executed contract, approvals, variations, and related purchase orders are retained in the contract repository.

### 5.2 Ownership

PostgreSQL owns approval and execution records. The contract repository owns the signed document, with the contract identifier referenced from the execution record.

### 5.3 Retention

Contracts are retained for at least 7 years after expiry or termination, unless a longer legal requirement applies.
