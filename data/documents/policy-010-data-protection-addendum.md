# Data Protection Addendum Requirements

## 1. Applicability

### 1.1 Personal data

A data protection addendum is required when a supplier processes personal data on behalf of the organisation, including names, contact details, identity records, and employee data.

### 1.2 No personal data

A supplier that only supplies goods without accessing personal data may use the standard contract without a separate addendum.

### 1.3 Responsibility

Legal owns the addendum template. Procurement must not approve a personal-data purchase without the addendum or an approved equivalent.

## 2. Required terms

### 2.1 Purpose limitation

The supplier may process personal data only for the documented purpose and only on the organisation's instructions.

### 2.2 Confidentiality

Personnel who access personal data must be bound by confidentiality and trained on the supplier's data protection obligations.

### 2.3 Security

The supplier must apply the security requirements in POL-005, including encryption, access control, and incident notification.

### 2.4 Subprocessors

A subprocessor requires written authorisation. The supplier remains responsible for the subprocessor's compliance and must provide notice of changes.

### 2.5 Data location

Personal data must remain in approved locations. A transfer outside the approved location requires legal and security approval.

## 3. Individual rights

### 3.1 Assistance

The supplier must assist with access, correction, deletion, and objection requests within 5 business days of an organisation request.

### 3.2 Deletion

At contract end, the supplier must return or delete personal data within 30 calendar days unless a legal retention requirement applies.

### 3.3 Certification of deletion

The supplier must provide written confirmation of deletion on request.

## 4. Incident response

### 4.1 Notification

A personal-data breach must be reported within 24 hours of discovery, consistent with POL-005.

### 4.2 Breach record

The report must include the categories and approximate number of affected people, the likely consequences, and the measures taken.

### 4.3 Cooperation

The supplier must cooperate with regulator or affected-person communication led by the organisation.

## 5. Audit and termination

### 5.1 Audit

The organisation may audit the supplier's data protection controls on reasonable notice, subject to confidentiality.

### 5.2 Termination

A material data protection breach permits termination for cause and may require suspension of processing.

### 5.3 Return of data

The supplier must return data in a commonly used, machine-readable format when requested.

## 6. Records

### 6.1 Contract reference

The addendum reference is recorded with the contract and the supplier ID in the contract repository.

### 6.2 Retention

Data protection records are retained for at least 7 years after the contract ends, unless a longer legal requirement applies.

### 6.3 Conflict

If the addendum conflicts with an active policy, the stricter protection applies.
